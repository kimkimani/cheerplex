## Cheerplex Code for Ethical Sports Wagering & Risk Awareness

1. **Strictly 18+ Participation:** Wagering in the Republic of Kenya is strictly prohibited for individuals under the age of 18. Cheerplex enforces active age verification across all services.
2. **Never Bet with Essential Capital:** Never wager funds needed for food, housing, healthcare, tuition, or essential living obligations. Treat subscriptions as recreational analytical entertainment.
3. **Never Chase Losses:** Outcomes in sports inherently contain random variance. If an accumulator or jackpot slip fails, do not escalate your stake to recover lost funds.
4. **Define Your Session & Stake Limits:** Establish pre-determined daily and weekly betting limits and never exceed them under emotional pressure.
