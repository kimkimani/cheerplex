## Frequently Asked Questions - 1X2 Predictions

### What does 1X2 mean in football betting?
In 1X2 betting, **1** represents a Home Win, **X** represents a Draw, and **2** represents an Away Win at the end of regular 90 minutes plus stoppage time.

### How does Cheerplex achieve high accuracy on 1X2 tips?
We combine dynamic Elo ratings, expected goals (xG), venue performance splits, and injury reports to compute accurate probability distributions for every outcome.

### Can I combine 1X2 predictions into multibets?
Yes! You can combine 2 to 3 high-confidence 1X2 banker selections into a compact accumulator, or explore our curated [Decimal Odds Packs](/cheerplex-sure-tips-and-odds/) for pre-built slips.

### What is the difference between 1X2 and Double Chance?
A 1X2 bet requires choosing exactly one outcome (1, X, or 2). A Double Chance bet covers two outcomes simultaneously (1X, X2, or 12), significantly increasing win probability with slightly lower odds.
